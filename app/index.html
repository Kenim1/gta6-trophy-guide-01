      Roadmap to the platinum

      Three stages, in order. Published guides disagree on the total time — PowerPyx and Push Square put it around 200 hours, other estimates run past 300 — so treat the hours below as a range, not a promise. The order is what matters.

      Overall: 0 of 55 earned

      Stage 1 is the story (Chapters 1–4 and the final mission). Stage 2 is the 100% checklist. Stage 3 is cleanup: the small jobs that are fastest once free roam is fully open.

      Missables — only one to worry about

      ⚠ ONE potentially missable trophy: Positive Mental Attitude. The first meditation spot is the balcony with Lucia after the mission Down to Earth, in Chapter 1. Meditate there before moving on — that moment does not come back.

      Everything else on the list can be earned in free roam after the story, including every mission, stranger strand, collectible and activity. No other trophy in the official list is tied to a one-time moment.

      Why nothing else is missable

      • Story & chapter trophies pop when their mission ends; missions are part of the main story, not one-time events.
      • Side-character finales (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone) unlock by taking that character's missions — they stay available in free roam.
      • Collectibles & counts (cards, WAiNK It, vehicles, areas, vaults, challenges) are cumulative and stay open after the finale.
      • Activities (races, drone races, shooting range, fishing, bowling, arcade) can be replayed any time.

      Sources & method

      Names, descriptions and grades are exactly as PlayStation publishes them, as listed by Exophase and PSNProfiles, who agree on every name, description and grade. Each guide in this app is written in our own words for this entry and names its sources beside it — PowerPyx (GTA 6 Trophy Guide & Roadmap), Push Square (GTA 6 Trophy Guide & Roadmap), Exophase (GTA 6 trophy list), PSNProfiles (GTA 6 trophy list) and the official PlayStation trophy list. No guide text is copied from another guide; sources are cited, never copied.

      Where sources disagree, this guide says so in the trophy's own entry — for example the total time to platinum (about 200 hours in some guides, 300+ in others) and the grade of Maximum Withdrawal (silver on the official list). This app fetches nothing at run time: it is static files only, works with the network off, and your progress never leaves this browser except when you export it yourself.

      How to use it: tick trophies as you earn them, use Next up for what to do first, use the search box to find any trophy by name, description, guide or source, and use Export / Import to move your progress to another browser.
