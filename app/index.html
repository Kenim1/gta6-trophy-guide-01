      Roadmap to the platinum — 3 stages, about 200–300 hours

      PowerPyx estimates about 200 hours to platinum; Push Square says 300 or more. This guide plans for 200–300. There are no difficulty settings, no mission gold medals, and no mission replay — so keep a manual save at the start and end of each chapter (15 manual slots, 1 autosave). Cheats disable trophies and saving.

      Missable trophies — 1 of 55

      Both guides we checked agree: only one trophy is potentially missable, and even that one is unconfirmed as required. Everything else can be earned after the story in free-roam, including every quest giver, who becomes available again later.

      Why nothing else is missable

      • Optional missions that disappear do not affect any trophy — there are far more than the 100 needed for the 100% checklist.
      • Quest givers who go quiet mid-story (Kenton, for example) return later; all trophy questlines stay open after the story.
      • Some fences and magazine collectibles in story buildings are lost, but neither affects a trophy — new fences unlock and the magazines also spawn elsewhere.
      • Time-sensitive quests with an hourglass icon fail if ignored, but none of them is tied to a trophy.
