      Roadmap to the platinum

      Five stages, in order. About 200 hours in total, difficulty 4/10, one playthrough — that is what PowerPyx, GTABase and KeenGamer agree on. Push Square says 300 hours or more, so treat the hours as a range, not a promise. The order is what matters. No mission replay and no gold medals for missions; keep a manual save at the start and end of each chapter (15 manual slots plus one autosave, per PowerPyx).

      Missable Watch — 9 trophies with a last-chance chapter

      Only one trophy can actually be missed. The other eight are story missions with no replay, so their chapter is their last chance to play them — keep a chapter save and you are safe.

      Missables — one to worry about, nine to watch

      ⚠ ONE potentially missable trophy: Positive Mental Attitude. The first meditation spot is the balcony with Lucia after the mission Down to Earth, in Chapter 1. Meditate there before moving on — that moment does not come back. Whether the trophy actually needs that spot is still unconfirmed (PowerPyx and GTABase both say so), so do it anyway: it costs minutes and protects the trophy either way.

      Everything else on the list can be earned in free roam after the story, including every mission, stranger strand, collectible and activity. No other trophy in the official list is tied to a one-time moment.

      Story gates, chapter by chapter

      91 main story missions across 5 chapters, per PC Gamer, GameRant and Hudwire, who agree on the main-mission counts. Optional-mission counts differ by source (IGN counts more early errands; GameTrek counts them separately), so only main missions are listed here.

      Why nothing else is missable

      • Story & chapter trophies pop when their mission ends; missions are part of the main story, not one-time events.
      • Side-character finales (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone) unlock by taking that character's missions — they stay available in free roam.
      • Collectibles & counts (cards, WAiNK It, vehicles, areas, vaults, challenges) are cumulative and stay open after the finale.
      • Activities (races, drone races, shooting range, fishing, bowling, arcade) can be replayed any time.

      Sources & method

      Names, descriptions and grades are exactly as PlayStation publishes them, as listed by Exophase, PSNProfiles and TrueTrophies, who agree on all 55. Each guide in this app is written in our own words for this entry and names its sources beside it. No guide text is copied from another guide; sources are cited, never copied. Every source below was read in this session on 20 Nov 2026.

      How certainty works: Confirmed means two or more sources agree. Disputed–resolved means sources disagreed and the majority or the official list settled it — the dispute is shown, not hidden. Still disputed means sources still disagree. Unconfirmed means no source has pinned the exact method yet. Where anything is disputed or unconfirmed, the trophy gives the safe action: the thing to do that earns the trophy either way.

      Honest limit: GTA 6 released 19 Nov 2026. Some trophy details cannot be 100% certain until players verify them in-game, and this guide never invents a count, chapter or requirement it did not read in a source.
